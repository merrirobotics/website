<h2>We love robots! And...</h2>
We are an up-and-coming robotics club based in Coburg High School in Merri Bek. Our club is aiming to participate in the FRC, the First Robotics Competition, and we are team number 12005. We are open to anyone in year 9 or above, including adults. 
<br><br>
We are here to provide experience in STEM and;
<br><br>
Make awesome robots<br>
Compete with other teams<br>
Learn practical engineering skills<br>
Work as team<br>
And have fun!<br>
<br>
