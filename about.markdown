We love robots! And...

We are an up-and-coming robotics club based in Coburg High School in Merri Bek. Our club is aiming to participate in the FRC, the First Robotics Competition, and we are team number 11402. We are open to anyone in year 9 or above, including adults. 

We are here to provide experience in STEM and;

    Make awesome robots
    Compete with other teams
    Learn practical engineering skills
    Work as team
    And have fun!

If you would like to join us, contact us at coburgroboticsfrc@gmail.com
